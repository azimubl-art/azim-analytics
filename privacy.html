Privacy Policy

App: Azim YouTube Analytics
Owner: MD Azim Khan
Last updated: October 30, 2026

1. About this app

Azim YouTube Analytics is a personal tool used by its owner to view the performance of his own YouTube channel, "Azim's Travel and Photography". It is not a public service and has no other users.

2. Data the app accesses

After the owner signs in with his Google account and gives permission, the app reads, in read-only mode, the YouTube Analytics data of the owner's own channel: daily views, watch time, average view duration, and subscribers gained and lost. The app cannot change, upload, delete or publish anything on the channel.

3. How the data is used

The data is used only to show the channel's performance to the owner. It is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

4. Sharing of data

The app does not sell, rent or share this data with any third party.

5. Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Access tokens and security

To keep access working, the app stores an authorization token for the owner's account on a private server. The token is not shared with anyone.

7. Revoking access and deleting data

The owner can remove the app's access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. After access is removed, the app can no longer read any data from the channel.
