Azim YouTube Analytics

Azim YouTube Analytics is a small personal tool created by MD Azim Khan, the creator of the YouTube channel Azim's Travel and Photography.

The tool reads the owner's own YouTube Analytics (daily views, watch time, average view duration and subscribers gained or lost) so that the owner can review the performance of his own channel. It is not offered to the public and is used only by its owner.
